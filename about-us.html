Our story

4K Projects was born from lived experience. As designers ourselves, we knew the pain of CGI outsourcing all too well – missed deadlines, poor communication, and having to spoon-feed requirements to teams who simply didn't get it.

We'd faced these frustrations project after project, so we decided to build the solution we needed.

Today, we're a team of 20+ skilled artists, led by experienced interior designers who've been exactly where you are now. They understand workplace design challenges, speak your language, and know exactly what you need without lengthy explanations.

We've built the studio we wished we'd had access to – one that delivers exceptional quality without the usual headaches. Our project managers aren't just coordinators; they're former designers who understand your pressures, deadlines, and client expectations.

We've been in your shoes, and we know what truly matters.
